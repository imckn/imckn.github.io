PDE - partial differential equation

single cell population

$$

  1. \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t}=D\nabla^2\rho+r\rho
    $$

$$
2. \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t}=D\nabla^2\rho+r\rho(1-\frac{\rho}{k})
$$

$(1-\frac{\rho}{k})$ -> logistic model
$$
3. \frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}=D\nabla^2\rho-\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\alpha\rho\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial x})
$$

$$
4. \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t}=D\nabla^2\rho-\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\alpha\rho\frac{\partial \rho}{\partial x})+r\rho
$$

two subpopulation

$$
5. \frac{\partial \rho_l}{\partial t}=D_l \nabla^2\rho_l-\alpha_l\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\rho_l\frac{\partial \rho_l}{\partial x})+r\rho_l
$$

$$
6. \frac{\partial\rho_l}{\partial t}=D_l\nabla^2\rho_l-\alpha_l\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\rho_l\frac{\partial\rho_l}{\partial x})-\alpha_{lf}\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\rho_l\frac{\partial\rho_f}{\partial x})
$$

$$
7.\frac{\partial\rho_l}{\partial t}=D_l\nabla^2\rho_l-\alpha1 … \alpha_{fl}+r_l\rho_l+\nabla^4\rho_l
$$

PSD - power spectral density

every single cell has a trait triplet:

  1. Adhesion: How sticky the cell is.
  2. Migration: How strongly the cell pulls outward. (chemotaxis)
  3. Proliferation: How fast the cell grows and divides.

Two Hypotheses:

  1. The “Born” hypothesis: Leader cells are genetically destined to be leaders.
  2. The “Made” hypothesis: Any cell can become a leader if it happens to be pushed to the outer edge of the tumor and senses the right environmental signals (like hypoxia).
