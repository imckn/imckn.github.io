例如这个句子

在想不出合适的形容词时，可以考虑别的方向。

例如develop是动词，后面是名词，该空可以是形容词修饰后面的名词，也可以是介词

该题型难度不大。但是要注意填入的词什么词性都有可能，例如（were, or, to）也可能是答案。另外，要注意among, within等介词的用法

some examples for informal and formal tone:

  1. informal tone: Do you want to collborate on the research project?

​ formal tone: I’m writing to propose a potential collboration on this research project, should your time permit.

​ 其中，should your time permit是非常正式、客气的书面语法，相当于if your time permits

  1. informal: Can I present my research at the conference?

    formal : I wish to formally apply for an opportunity to present my current research findings at [conference name].

    can I -> I wish to formally apply for an opportunity to …

  2. informal: Can I use the lab equipment next week?

    formal: I would like to request access to the labotatory equipment for the week of [date].

    can I -> I wish to formally request access to ….

  3. informal: can you review my draft and give some feedback?

    formal: I would be grateful if you could provide feedback on my draft at your earliest convenience.

    can you -> I would be grateful if you could …

    give feedback -> provide feedback

道歉信写法：

I sincerely apologize for … (detailed matters) and any inconvenience this may have caused.

道歉信的example：

![apologize letter question example](C:\Users\kaina\OneDrive\apologize letter question example.png)

Dear Dr. Williams,

I hope this email finds you well.

I’m writing to explain why I missed the deadline for submitting last week’s lab report. I was suffering from a high fever over the past few days, which prevented me from completing the lab report on time. I also contacted the TA, but unfortunately, I haven’t received any response yet.

I sincerely apologize for the late submission and any inconvenience this may have caused. I understand how important it is to submit assignments on time. I would like to take full responsibility for the delay.

I would greatly appreciate it if you could allow me to submit the lab report late. If possible, I would like to request partial credit for the assigment.

Best regards,

Littlehill
